# Unpad privacy policy

Unpad sends the title and extracted text of a supported page, and sometimes its origin and path, to the service you connect. It does this when you turn cuts on for a page or enable automatic cuts on its site. It sends your **About you** notes with requests that use ChatGPT or an API key. The service you choose handles the request under its own privacy policy and terms.

ChatGPT and API key connections send requests directly from Chrome. Unpad operates no server and records no reading activity with these connections. Chrome keeps credentials, settings, and cached sentence selections on your device. Credentials are available only to trusted extension contexts. Disconnect in settings to remove your key or end your ChatGPT session. Uninstall the extension to remove its local settings and cache.

If you configure a bridge in **Advanced**, page text goes to the bridge address you enter. The bridge can send text to its configured model and store page text, selected sentences, and reading events in its data directory. The bridge operator controls that data.
